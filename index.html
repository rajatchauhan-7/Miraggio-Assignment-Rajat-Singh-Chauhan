The Cultural Insight
Indian women aged 22–32 no longer treat perfume as occasion-wear. It is now a daily ritual of self-declaration. Scent has replaced jewellery as the most personal luxury. Invisible yet felt by every room she enters. The rise of "scent shelfie" culture on Instagram and TikTok-led "what I smell like" content has made fragrance the #1 discovery category in Indian beauty.
03. Big Creative Idea
The Central Campaign Thought
Your Scent Is Your Signature
In a world full of copies, Miraggio stands for the original. Just as every Miraggio bag tells the story of the woman who carries it. Every Miraggio perfume tells the story of the woman who wears it. Not a borrowed identity from a European legacy house. Not a celebrity's name on a bottle. Yours. Distinctly Indian. Fiercely aspirational. Utterly personal.
Resonance
Miraggio's 700K+ existing customers already identify with the brand's "fashion-forward, accessible luxury" DNA. The perfume is a natural extension. Same woman, same ambition, new sensory territory.
Differentiation
Global brands sell aspiration to be someone else. Miraggio sells confidence to be yourself. Louder. In a market dominated by Chanel's French codes and SKINN's utilitarian positioning, this emotional lane is wide open.
Scalability
The idea works across every format: a 15-second Reel, a 3-minute brand film, a Myntra product page, a pop-up activation, a seasonal limited edition. One idea. Infinite executions.
04. Launch Campaign Concept
Campaign Name
Sillage
The trace you leave behind
Sillage (French: see-yazh): the trail a perfume leaves in the air as you walk past. In perfumery, it's the highest compliment: "Her sillage made the room pause."

For Miraggio's Indian audience, this is immediately powerful: What do you leave behind when you walk into a room? Your work. Your presence. Your scent. Your story. This is modern aspiration. Not about having more, but about being more felt.
✦
Phase I: Whisper
4-week teaser. No product reveal. Only scent stories. "What did she leave behind?" Influencers share personal "sillage moments" which are the traces they want to leave in their world. Maximum curiosity, zero product shots.
◈
Phase II: Reveal
Day 1 launch. Simultaneous drop across Miraggio.com, Myntra, Nykaa. A 90-second brand film. 50 influencers unbox on the same day. PR fragrance send to 200 journalists and fashion editors. #MySillage goes live.
◇
Phase III: Own
30-day sustain. UGC flood. "What's your Sillage?" challenge. Pop-up scent bars in 4 cities. Limited collector's edition bottle. Gifting season push (Diwali / birthday / corporate). Repeat purchase programme activated.

06. Content & Storytelling
Content that
you feel, not just see.
Brand Film · 90 Seconds
"The Room Remembered Her"
A woman walks through five different rooms: a boardroom, a café, an airport, a wedding, a rooftop. Then she leaves. Each room pauses for a beat after she's gone. The only thing that lingers: her scent. No dialogue. Only music, movement, and the universal human truth that how you're remembered matters as much as how you arrive. Closes on the bottle. "Sillage by Miraggio."
Instagram Reels · Series
"Name My Sillage"
5-part creator series where influencers describe their sillage, not their perfume, but what they want to leave behind. "I want to leave curiosity." "I want to leave warmth." "I want to leave power." Final reel: "We made a perfume for each of you." Each scent in the Miraggio line is named after a feeling, not a French word.
Launch Teaser · 2 Weeks Pre-Drop
"Coming: Your Signature"
Blurred, smoky visuals of the bottle. No logo, no brand name for the first 10 days. Just the question: "What does she leave behind?" Countdown. Then 4 days before launch: the Miraggio logo drops. Designed to generate maximum speculation within the existing community of 700K customers who will be teased first via email and WhatsApp.
User-Gen Content Activation
#MySillage Challenge
Launch-day UGC drive: Post a Reel of your sillage moment: the version of you that walks into a room and makes it pause. Tag Miraggio. Best stories win a personalised bottle with their name engraved. Estimated 10K–50K organic UGC posts in first 30 days based on comparable D2C fashion brand launches in India.
Visual Storytelling · Product Page
Scent Notes as Emotions
Each fragrance's product page abandons generic "top note: bergamot" language. Instead: "Opens with the confidence of a woman who knows her worth. Settles into the warmth of a slow Sunday morning. The base note is the version of you that everyone remembers." Converts clinical product copy into aspirational identity copy.
YouTube · Long-Form
"The Making of Miraggio's Signature"
A 6-minute documentary on how Miraggio worked with a master perfumer to create "the first perfume designed for the modern Indian woman's ambitions, not her occasion." Behind-the-scenes in the lab. Founder's voice. The cultural brief given to the perfumer. Builds prestige and authenticity. It is the same playbook Dior uses for J'adore's behind-the-scenes content, adapted for India.
07. Visual Identity & Moodboard
The aesthetic
language of Sillage.
Aa
Typography
Cormorant Garamond (serif, italic headlines) + Montserrat (body). Evokes Old Money meets New India.
Colour World

⬡

Minimalist rectangular silhouette. Gold-dipped collar. Frosted glass in nude-beige. Designed to live on a dresser, not hide in a drawer.
◈

Warm, candlelit. Natural skin tones. No euro-centric casting. Indian women of different complexions. Confidence is the only common thread.
08. Launch Roadmap
Twelve weeks.
Three acts.
Weeks 1–4 · Act I
Whisper: The Tease
  • Email + WhatsApp teaser to 700K existing Miraggio customers: "Something new is coming."
  • Blurred bottle content drops on Instagram. No hashtag, no explanation. Just: "What do you leave behind?"
  • 10 macro influencers seeded with product under NDA. Their silence builds the buzz.
  • PR kit dispatched to 200 fashion journalists and beauty editors across India
  • Behind-the-scenes "making of" footage shot and held for YouTube drop at launch
Week 5 · Act II
Reveal: Launch Day
  • 9AM: Email blast to 700K+ base. "Sillage by Miraggio is here."
  • 9AM: Live on Miraggio.com, Myntra, Nykaa, Amazon simultaneously
  • 10AM: 50 influencers post unboxing simultaneously. #MySillage goes live
  • 12PM: Brand film drops on YouTube + Instagram + paid amplification
  • Founder interview live on Instagram Stories, fully authentic and unscripted
  • Myntra "New Launch" feature placement + Nykaa "Trending" banner secured
Weeks 6–12 · Act III
Own: Sustain and Scale
  • #MySillage UGC challenge: Top 10 entries win personalised engraved bottles
  • Pop-up scent bars in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad, four days each
  • Diwali gift-box edition: Limited collector's packaging in festive gold
  • Retargeting campaign to Miraggio bag buyers who haven't yet converted to perfume
  • YouTube documentary: "The Making of Sillage", a 6-minute brand film, drops in Week 8
  • Repeat purchase incentive: "Your second bottle, personalised."
